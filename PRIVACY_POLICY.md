# IthmiaMCLink — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 15, 2026  
**Last updated:** November 15, 2026

## 1. Who Operates IthmiaMCLink

IthmiaMCLink (the **Bot**) is operated by the IthmiaMC community administrator (the **Operator**) to connect the IthmiaMC Discord server and Minecraft server (`play.ithmiamc.quest`).

**Privacy contact:** arborealguitarist@gmail.com

This policy describes data processed by the Bot and its associated Minecraft plugins. Discord, Microsoft/Minecraft, and hosting providers also process data under their own policies.

## 2. Information Processed

Depending on the features you use and which options the Operator enables, the Bot or server may process:

| Data | Why it is needed |
| --- | --- |
| Discord user ID and relevant server/member information, such as username, nickname, and roles | Identify users, process commands, and manage authorized Discord roles |
| Minecraft UUID (or Floodgate-associated identifier), Minecraft username, and permission group | Identify the Minecraft account and coordinate linked roles |
| Discord ID–Minecraft ID association | Keep accounts linked until unlinked or deleted |
| Temporary account-linking codes and command interactions | Verify that the person initiating a link controls the intended accounts |
| Messages posted in configured Discord and Minecraft bridge channels, along with sender and channel information | Relay messages across platforms and allow community moderation |
| Events such as player joins, leaves, deaths, advancements, and moderation actions | Display enabled community notifications and troubleshoot misuse |
| Operational and security logs, which may include timestamps, usernames, identifiers, command events, and network information generated by the hosting infrastructure | Maintain functionality, security, and abuse prevention |

The Bot is not intended to access private Discord direct messages unrelated to its commands, or messages in channels where it has no access. Discord or Minecraft may nevertheless hold separate copies of content under their own practices.

**Do not enter passwords, authentication tokens, addresses, or other sensitive personal information in bridged channels or Bot commands.**

## 3. How We Use Information

We use this information to:

- Connect Discord and Minecraft chat in designated channels.
- Process voluntary account linking and unlinking.
- Synchronize approved Minecraft groups with Discord roles where configured.
- Provide enabled community events, player information, or messaging features.
- Operate, secure, debug, and moderate the service.
- Comply with valid legal obligations.

We do not use account-linking information for targeted advertising or sell it to data brokers. **This statement must remain true in actual operation.**

## 4. When Information Is Shared

Information is shared only as needed to provide the service or where required by law:

- **Discord:** Bridged Minecraft messages and configured server events are sent to Discord and may be accessible to members with access to the destination channel. Discord processes this content under its own policies.
- **Minecraft participants:** Messages from configured Discord channels may be shown to players currently connected to the Minecraft server.
- **Service providers:** Minecraft hosting, server infrastructure, and applicable software/platform providers may process data needed to host or transmit the service.
- **Moderators:** Authorized IthmiaMC staff may access messages, links, roles, and relevant logs as needed for community administration and security.
- **Legal or safety requests:** Information may be disclosed if required by applicable law or to address credible threats or abuse.

The Bot does not make linked account identifiers publicly searchable as a standalone service. However, the usernames and roles displayed through ordinary Discord/Minecraft features may be visible to other community participants.

## 5. Retention and Deletion

Account-linking records are maintained while a link remains active and are removed from the active linking system when an account is successfully unlinked, subject to any necessary operational or legal retention. Messages sent into Discord may remain in Discord's channel history until removed under Discord/server retention practices; unlinking does **not** automatically erase messages already relayed there.

Operational logs, backups, and moderation records may persist separately from the active account link. The Operator should publish and follow actual retention settings and honor valid deletion requests. **Before publication, confirm the retention and backup practices for the Minecraft host, any Discord console/log channels, and EssentialsX storage.**

## 6. Security

The Operator takes reasonable measures to protect Bot credentials and restrict administrative access. No online service can promise absolute security. Please report suspected unauthorized account linking or other security problems through the contact channel above.

## 7. Children and Age Requirements

The Discord features of IthmiaMCLink are intended only for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirements in their jurisdiction. The Operator does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children who are too young to use Discord. If you believe such information was collected, contact the Operator so it can be reviewed and removed as appropriate.
